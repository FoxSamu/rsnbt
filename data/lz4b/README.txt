The lz4b files here were generated using the same library that Minecraft
uses for LZ4Block compression. Their decompressed contents are the respective
txt files.

                    Block size: 64. Compression level: Fast(1).

lz4yap.lz4b     =   A long yapping seesion about LZ4 compression and why it is
                    so useful. Courtesy of Claude lol.
                    Block size: 4096. Compression level: Fast(1).

The files are used for testing the LZ4Block decoder and encoder.
